Hunt: Showdown 1896 – A Review

The moment-to-moment gameplay, when the game decides it’d like to function properly, is great; but, if this game is at all representative of the technical prowess & overall stability of CRYENGINE, then Crytek should be wholly ashamed. And well, if not, then they should be doubly ashamed for producing this garbage on their “world-class” engine as they call it.

I’d nonetheless recommend giving it a try. Even though it’ll likely frustrate you due to technical issues and convoluted non-gameplay design, as I’ve said, the moment-to-moment gameplay is actually quite well-made, and more importantly, fun.
